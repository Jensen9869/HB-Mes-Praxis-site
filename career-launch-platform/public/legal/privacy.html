Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 Nov 2026 · Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BirthdayMessaging ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal data when you visit the Career Launch Platform at praxis.birthdaymessaging.io, apply to a Programme or take part in one. We are the data controller for this data.

Contact: BirthdayMessaging, 15 Forest Road, Erith, Kent DA8 2NS, United Kingdom · support@birthdaymessaging.io

1. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Full name, email address, university/college/school, expected graduation yearWhen you submit an applicationTo process your application, contact you and administer your enrolment

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on the Platform, and we do not buy data about you from third parties.

2. Legal bases

  • Contract — most processing is necessary to assess your application and deliver the Programme you have paid for.
  • Legal obligation — keeping payment and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests — securing the Platform, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving the Programmes, where this does not override your rights.
  • Consent — if we ever want to showcase your work publicly or send you marketing, we will ask first, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers who process it on our instructions, and only as needed:

  • PayPal — processes your payments. PayPal is an independent controller for the payment itself; see PayPal's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the Platform and its database and protects it from attacks.
  • Our email service — sends application, payment and sign-in emails from careers.birthdaymessaging.io.
  • Mentors and Programme staff — see the information needed to support you (name, email, track, your work).

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. International transfers

We are based in the United Kingdom. Our providers (PayPal, Cloudflare) may process data in the United States or other countries. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses so that it stays protected.

5. How long we keep data

  • Applications that are never paid for: deleted 12 months after submission.
  • Enrolment and Programme records: for the length of the Programme and 2 years after it ends.
  • Payment records: 6 years after the tax year of the payment, as required by UK law.
  • Sign-in tokens: 15 minutes; session cookies: 7 days.
  • Support emails: 2 years.

6. Cookies

The Platform sets one cookie, session, only after you sign in to the student portal. It is strictly necessary, lasts 7 days and is not used for tracking or advertising. No consent banner is needed for strictly necessary cookies. If you clear it you will simply need a new sign-in link.

7. Security

All traffic to the Platform is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time links instead of passwords, so there is no password for us to lose. Access to student data is limited to staff who need it. Payment details are handled entirely by PayPal.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise a right, email support@birthdaymessaging.io from your enrolled email address. We respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Children

If you are under 18, please apply with the knowledge and agreement of a parent or guardian. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must contact us before you apply so that we can obtain their consent. If we learn that we hold data about a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it.

10. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will also email you.
