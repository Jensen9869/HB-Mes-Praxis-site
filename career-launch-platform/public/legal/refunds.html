Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 19 Nov 2026 · Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

This policy explains what happens to your payments if you stop, withdraw from or are not accepted onto a Programme on the Career Launch Platform. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions. Please read it before you pay.

In short
  • Monthly plans: you can stop future instalments at any time. Instalments already paid are not refunded.
  • Upfront payments: not refunded once the Programme has started.
  • Your 14-day statutory cancellation right is always respected (see section 1).
  • If we decline your application, your payment is refunded in full.

1. Your 14-day cancellation right

If you are a consumer, you have a legal right to cancel your enrolment within 14 days of the day you enrol (the day your first payment is confirmed), for any reason.

By enrolling you ask us to begin your Programme within that 14-day period. If you cancel during the 14 days after your Programme has started, we will refund your payment less a proportionate amount for the part of the Programme delivered up to the date you told us you wanted to cancel. If you cancel before the Programme start date, you receive a full refund.

To cancel within the 14 days, email support@birthdaymessaging.io from the email address you enrolled with, stating that you wish to cancel. Refunds under this section are made to the PayPal account you paid from within 14 days of your cancellation.

2. Monthly instalment plans

Monthly plans are a fixed-term commitment (12 months for the Apprenticeship Programme; 3 or 6 months for Mentorship) paid in monthly instalments through PayPal. The plan ends automatically after the final instalment and does not renew.

  • Stopping future payments. You may cancel your PayPal subscription at any time from your PayPal account, or by emailing us. No further instalments will be taken after cancellation.
  • Payments already made are not refunded, including the instalment for the month in which you cancel. You keep access to the Programme until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • No pro-rata refunds are given for partial months or for sessions, assignments or materials you chose not to use.
  • Re-joining later requires a new application at the fees current at that time.

3. Upfront payments

If you paid the full Programme fee upfront at the discounted upfront price, that payment is non-refundable once the Programme has started, other than under your 14-day cancellation right (section 1) or where section 5 applies. If you withdraw part-way through, no refund or credit is given for the remaining months.

4. Applications we decline

If we are unable to offer you a place after reviewing your application, we will cancel your PayPal subscription and refund any payment you have made in full. Refunds are processed manually by our team through PayPal and normally appear within 5–10 business days of our decision email.

5. If we cancel or materially change a Programme

If we withdraw a Programme, or materially reduce what you have paid for, and you do not wish to continue, we will refund the fees you have paid for the part of the Programme not delivered. This includes a proportionate refund of an upfront payment.

6. Failed payments

If a monthly instalment fails, PayPal will retry it automatically and we will email you. Access to the Programme is paused while an instalment is outstanding. If the instalment is still unpaid after PayPal's retry attempts, your subscription will be suspended and we may cancel your enrolment. Instalments already paid are not refunded.

7. Removal for breach of our Terms

If we end your enrolment because you have seriously or repeatedly breached our Terms and Conditions (for example academic dishonesty, abusive conduct or sharing Programme materials), no refund is given for payments already made and any upfront payment is forfeited.

8. How refunds are paid

All refunds are made through PayPal to the PayPal account used for the original payment, in US dollars. We are not responsible for differences in the amount you receive caused by currency conversion or your bank's fees. Please do not open a PayPal dispute or card chargeback before contacting us; we aim to resolve refund questions within 5 business days.

9. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 or any other law that cannot be excluded. If you live outside the United Kingdom, you may also have rights under the consumer laws of your country of residence.

10. Contact

Questions about cancellation or refunds: support@birthdaymessaging.io. Please include the email address you enrolled with and the Programme name.
