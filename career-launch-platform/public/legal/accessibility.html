Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 19 Nov 2026 · Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

BirthdayMessaging wants everyone to be able to use the Career Launch Platform (praxis.birthdaymessaging.io) and take part in our Programmes, regardless of ability or technology.

1. Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The Platform is built with semantic HTML, labelled form fields, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, sufficient colour contrast and text that scales with your browser settings.

2. Known limitations

  • Payment is completed on PayPal's website, whose accessibility we do not control.
  • Some mentor-provided materials (for example PDFs or third-party videos) may not yet be fully accessible. Tell us and we will provide an alternative format.

3. Help and adjustments

If you have difficulty using any part of the Platform, applying, paying, or taking part in a Programme, email support@birthdaymessaging.io. Tell us what you were trying to do and what went wrong. We will respond within 5 business days and, where we can, offer an alternative way to complete the task or reasonable adjustments to your Programme.

4. Continuous improvement

We review the Platform for accessibility when we make significant changes and act on feedback we receive. This statement will be updated as we make improvements.
